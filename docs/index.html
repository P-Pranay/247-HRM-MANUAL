Start here

This manual is a starting point for the modules visible in the 247HRM demo portal. It is written for employees and managers, with notes for HR users where appropriate.

Access and permissions: Your organization’s setup determines which modules, records, and actions you can see. A missing menu item or button usually means it is not enabled for your role. Use your company’s HR or system administrator as the source of truth for policy and approval rules.

Menu labels and workflows can change between organizations and software releases. The attendance calendar, employee directory, payroll summary, and approvals dashboard were checked against the demo portal. Other module descriptions below are based on the visible menu names; use the prompts in your own portal for tenant-specific fields and rules.

Before you begin

  • Use the 247HRM address provided by your organization and sign in with your assigned account.
  • Do not share your password, one-time codes, payslips, employee records, or other sensitive HR information.
  • Check the employee, dates, pay period, and request type before submitting a request.
  • For actions affecting pay, employment status, or employee records, follow your internal approval process and verify the result before finishing.

Navigation & account basics

  1. Open the navigation menu. Use the menu button in the portal header to reveal the available modules and groups.
  2. Choose a module. The menu groups employee applications, HR tools, Tax, Performance and Growth, reports, and support links. Your menu may differ by role.
  3. Use dashboard shortcuts. The welcome dashboard may provide Quick Links such as Attendance, Employee Profile, Leave & Attendance, Payslip Download, and Approvals.
  4. Review your account options. The account menu includes password-change and logout options. Log out when using a shared device.
  5. Use on-screen help. Where available, the help icon opens guidance for the current screen. Read its rules and warnings before submitting high-impact actions.
Never leave an authenticated HR portal open on a public or shared computer. Follow your organization’s security and data-handling policies.

Employee self-service

Employee-facing tools are grouped under My Applications. They let you review personal HR information or submit requests. Submission is not the same as approval; check the request status or Approvals area afterward.

My Profile

Review your profile and available personal or employment details. If a value is wrong or read-only, contact HR rather than submitting a duplicate record.

Web Login

Record an attendance check-in or check-out, and review punch history. A web punch may capture a selfie and location; follow your organization’s network and attendance rules.

Employee Payslip

Open the payslip area, select the available period, review the statement, and download it using the provided action. Handle downloaded files as confidential.

Income Tax Saving

Review or submit tax-saving declarations and supporting details when the declaration window is open. Check the applicable tax year and evidence requirements first.

Resignation

Use this area only if you intend to formally submit a resignation request. Confirm the date and internal process with HR before submission.

Advances & Loans

Review or request an advance or loan through the relevant application. Check the amount, repayment terms, and approval status before proceeding.

Travel Requisition

Start a travel request using the fields provided by your organization. Submit it through the required approval route before making non-refundable bookings.

Leave Encashment

Review eligibility and the leave balance before requesting encashment. Availability and calculation rules are organization-specific.

Local Claim

Enter the claim details and attach supporting evidence if requested. Verify dates, amounts, and approval status; do not submit duplicate claims.

Compensatory Off

Review the available earned balance and apply or redeem it according to your company’s policy. The leave calendar may display it as C-Off or Comp Off.

Web Login: record an attendance punch

Use this workflow when you are ready to record your own attendance. The portal displays the current date and time, asks for a purpose, and may capture a selfie and location with the punch.

  1. Open Web Login from My Applications or the dashboard shortcut.
  2. Review the displayed date and time. Check that the selected purpose matches the action you intend to record.
  3. Add a note only if needed; the Notes field is optional on the observed screen.
  4. Choose Check-IN when starting work, or Check-OUT when ending work or recording an allowed logout punch.
  5. Review the confirmation prompt. Confirm only if the punch details and action are correct; otherwise cancel and correct the selection.
  6. After the action completes, review the punch or use View History to check recorded attendance.
A confirmed punch records attendance and may capture personal data such as a selfie and location. Do not confirm an unintended check-in or check-out. Access may be limited to approved networks, and purpose options can vary by organization.

Review Web Login attendance history

  1. Choose the month to review. Future months are not available.
  2. If you have manager access, select an employee; otherwise, the page shows your own attendance.
  3. Choose All, Web, or Bio Metric to filter punches by source.
  4. Select a day in the Punch Timeline, then select a punch to review its available details, such as purpose, notes, selfie, and map location.
  5. Use Employee Time Spent to view the productive and non-productive time chart when it is enabled for your organization.

Other employee tools

  • Help Desk: Raise or follow a support request using the available category and description fields.
  • Log Time: Record work time or project time if your role and project setup allow it.
  • Employee Assets: Review assigned equipment or asset information; report discrepancies to the responsible team.
  • Uploads: Use only for documents requested by the relevant workflow. Check file type and destination before uploading.
  • Company Policy: Read the current policy documents published for your organization.

Leave & attendance

The Leave & Attendance calendar shows attendance, leave, holidays, and related events for the selected employee and pay period. Your available actions depend on role and policy.

Review attendance or leave

  1. Open Leave & Attendance or the Attendance quick link.
  2. Confirm the employee and pay period. Managers with access may be able to select another employee.
  3. Use the calendar to review day-level events. Switch to the list or punch view to inspect attendance details where available.
  4. Use the event legend to filter categories. Open Summary for totals and Leave Balances to review balances.

Apply for leave

  1. Select a date on the calendar or choose Apply Leave.
  2. Select the leave type and date range. Review the displayed balance and calculated number of days.
  3. For a single-day half-day request, choose either First Half or Second Half if those options are shown. Do not select both.
  4. Enter the required reason. Add peers or an attachment only if useful or required by your policy.
  5. If an optional holiday is required, select the offered holiday date.
  6. Review all details and submit. Check the request status later; do not assume it is approved until the portal shows that status.

Attendance incidents and missing punches

Use the incident or correction flow—not a leave request—for issues such as a missing punch, on-tour status, shift change, or personal permission. Select the incident type and explain the correction. Missing-punch requests may require the swipe direction and time. Provide only accurate information.

Compensatory off

Compensatory off is earned time off for qualifying work on a holiday or weekly off. Confirm the balance and dates before applying. If the balance is a half day, the portal may require a First Half or Second Half selection.

The number of leave days is calculated by the system; it is not manually editable. Available leave types, optional holidays, half-day rules, and approval routes are controlled by your organization.

Related attendance views

  • Attendance Calendar: Calendar view of attendance, leave, and holidays.
  • Shift Summary: Shift-related summary, if enabled.
  • Leave Balances: Balance details such as opening, accrued, utilized, or available balance may be shown.
  • C-Off History: Compensatory-off history, if available.
  • Incident History: Previously submitted attendance corrections.
  • Leave, Incident, and C-Off Approvals: Approver views for pending requests.

Approvals

The Approvals dashboard centralizes employee-related requests. The demo portal showed categories including tax-saving details, leaves/incidents/C-Off, new joinees, employee updates, HR approval, resignation status changes, local claims, leave encashment, and final settlement.

Review a request

  1. Open Approvals from the navigation menu or dashboard.
  2. Choose the request category that matches the item you need to review.
  3. Open the pending item and check the employee, dates, amounts or details, comments, and attachments relevant to the request.
  4. Use the available action to approve, reject, or return the request only after applying your organization’s policy.
  5. Confirm the result and check whether another approval step remains.
Approval actions can affect employees, leave balances, or pay. Verify the record and your authority before acting. If the request is unclear, ask the requester or HR rather than approving by assumption.

HR operations

HR tools are intended for authorized HR users. The descriptions below summarize the visible menu; available actions and required approvals vary by organization.

Employee Master

Search, filter, sort, and open the employee directory. The default directory may show employed staff; change the status filter to find other lifecycle statuses. Open a card for quick details or the full profile. Add Employee and Bulk Upload may be permission-gated.

Prospective Employee

View or manage prospective-employee records where enabled. Confirm the correct record and recruitment/onboarding stage before updates.

Holiday Management

Review or maintain organization holidays if authorized. Confirm the applicable location and calendar year before publishing changes.

Letter Generation

Generate HR letters from available templates. Verify the employee, letter type, dates, and generated document before sharing it.

Employee ID Card

Open the ID-card area to create or review employee cards where permitted. Check identity and employment details before issuing.

Advances Management

Review or administer employee advance records. Check approvals, balances, and repayment details against policy.

Loans Management

Review or administer loan records. Verify principal, repayment schedule, employee, and approval status.

Leave Encashment by HR

Authorized HR users can review leave-encashment processing. Confirm eligibility and calculation rules before finalizing.

Arrears Calculation

Use for authorized arrears processing. Validate the affected employees, period, inputs, and resulting amounts before processing.

Final Settlement

Use for authorized separation settlement tasks. Confirm employment dates, dues, deductions, and approval requirements with HR policy.

Bonus Calculation

Review bonus calculations and eligible employees before any final action. Eligibility and calculation rules are organization-specific.

Exgratia Calculation

Review ex-gratia calculations for the applicable employees and period. Follow internal authorization and verification procedures.

Stop Salary

High-impact payroll control. Use only with explicit authorization and verified employee and effective period details.

Delete Document

Document deletion may be irreversible. Confirm retention requirements, ownership, and authorization before removing any file.

Employee directory: useful controls

  • Search matches employee records as you type; clear the search to restore the current directory view.
  • Use the status control to find employed, resigned, terminated, inactive, rejected, or approval-pending records where those statuses are available.
  • Use the advanced filter when you need more than a single status or sort order.
  • Employee cards may load progressively as you scroll. A selected card opens a quick-detail panel; use the full-profile action for the complete record.
  • If an action is missing, your account may not have the relevant permission. Do not use another person’s account to bypass access controls.

Payroll & tax

Payroll summary

The Payroll module summarizes a selected month and pay period. It may show gross pay, processed payroll, employees still to process, and component summaries such as attendance, leave, joiners, resignations, allowances, deductions, overtime, statutory items, tax, budget, or stopped salaries.

For employee self-service, use Employee Payslip or Payslips to find the appropriate statement. Do not use HR payroll-processing actions unless you are authorized to administer payroll.

Authorized payroll processing sequence

  1. Select the correct month and pay period. Payroll data depends on both.
  2. Review the summary and component exceptions. Resolve missing employee, attendance, leave, allowance, deduction, loan, advance, or tax inputs through the appropriate module.
  3. Run a trial payroll when available and review results before a final run.
  4. Complete attendance processing and review loss-of-pay (LOP) values and other exceptions.
  5. Calculate payroll, review the resulting figures, then follow your organization’s approval and lock process.
  6. After locking, treat the run as finalized. Corrections may require an authorized unlock and rerun process.
Payroll actions can affect compensation. The exact stages, approvals, and correction procedures are tenant-specific. Follow your payroll control checklist; do not treat this summary as authorization to process or lock payroll.

Payroll and tax-related menu items

  • Payroll: Authorized payroll summary and processing tools.
  • Payslips / Employee Payslip: Payroll statements for the relevant employee and period.
  • Income Tax Saving: Employee tax declarations and evidence, when available.
  • EMI: A tax-related menu item visible in the portal; follow the page instructions for applicable entries.
  • Arrears, Bonus, Exgratia, Stop Salary: Specialized HR/payroll processing tools; use only with the appropriate authorization.

Reports, rewards & help

  • Reports: Open only reports you are authorized to view. Apply the requested period or filter, confirm the scope, and handle exported files as confidential employee data.
  • Gifts & Rewards: Review the available recognition or rewards features. Eligibility and redemption rules depend on company policy.
  • 247 HRM Support: Use the support entry for product or portal assistance. Include the screen name and a clear description; do not include passwords or unnecessary personal data.
  • Notifications: Check the notification control in the header for alerts and updates. Open the related module to review the request or message.
  • Quick Links and dashboards: Dashboard shortcuts may include role-specific views such as HR, manager, or CXO dashboards, plus attendance, profile, leave, payslip, time-log, loan, advance, claim, and approval actions.

Troubleshooting

  • A module or action is missing: Check the navigation menu and your role permissions. Ask HR or the system administrator to confirm access.
  • No employee appears in the directory: Check the selected status filter and clear the search text. The default view may show only employed staff.
  • Leave cannot be submitted: Check the leave type, dates, required reason, half-day choice, available balance, and any optional-holiday requirement. The system calculates the day count.
  • Attendance details did not load: Use the page refresh or retry control if offered. If the issue continues, contact support with the page name and approximate time.
  • Payroll data is blank: Verify that both month and pay period are selected. Ask the payroll administrator if the period is not available.
  • A request is still pending: Submission does not equal approval. Check the request status and contact the assigned approver if necessary.
  • Upload or report problems: Verify the correct module, required fields, file type, and date range. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same request.
  • Portal errors continue: Note the screen, action, and time; capture a screenshot only if it contains no confidential information. Send the details to your internal support contact.

Glossary

Approval
A review decision required before a request or process is finalized.
C-Off / Comp-Off
Compensatory time off earned for qualifying work outside the normal schedule, subject to company policy.
LOP
Loss of Pay: unpaid time reflected in payroll according to configured rules.
Pay period
The organization-configured payroll cycle used to scope payroll or attendance data.
Pending
A request or action that has not yet reached a final status.
Trial payroll
A preview used by authorized payroll users to review figures before final processing.
